### Strategic Signals (This Quarter)
- **Google is consolidating two separate product lines — Vertex AI and Agentspace — under a single "Gemini" brand and, apparently, a single bill.** Read together, this week's two Tier-1/Tier-2 changes are not isolated: they're the same repositioning move (one platform, one agent, one invoice) surfacing on two different pages in the same week. That is a simplification play aimed at matching the "one bill" pitch AWS already makes with Bedrock, not a new capability.
- **Removing scale-to-zero while packaging multi-year CUD-stackable Savings Plans is a trade of granular cost control for a packaged commit vehicle.** It raises the honest-concession bar for AWS reps: Google now has a real multi-year AI commit analog, but it comes at the cost of idle-time flexibility that Bedrock's token-metered model still offers natively.
- **The agent-platform repositioning (Agentspace → single "Gemini" agent) is a bet on turnkey packaging — budget governance, 100+ pre-built connectors, one audit trail — over composability.** That is the opposite bet AWS is making with AgentCore/Strands/MCP (model-neutral, composable runtime). Both are credible positions; the honest framing for sellers is "turnkey vs. composable," not "Google has no agent story."
- **The source outage is now long enough that "unchanged" and "re-confirmed" remain different claims for a 12th straight week.** TPU pricing (tpu7x $13.80/chip-hr, v6e $3.105/hr) and TPU supply/availability, Cloud revenue/capex trajectory, and any GA/deprecation notice in `gcp_release_notes_all` all remain unconfirmed for three months running — treat any Ironwood/tpu7x throughput or GA claim repeated by a customer as unverified by this pipeline, not refreshed.
